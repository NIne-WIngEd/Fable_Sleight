01 / THE IDEA

PERSONAL FROM THE FOUNDATION UP

Not just what you know.
How you see the world.

Most of your life was never written down. A few files can’t tell your whole story. Sleight will use what you choose to share to learn patterns in how you think, then build a personal foundation around them.

Its memory, personality and judgment will develop together. General skills like coding and research can come from connected frontier models.

HOW IT WILL WORK

01Start with your world.+

Choose the files, conversations and records you want it to learn from. You decide what comes in.

02Let Sleight build.+

The builder will create and connect your personal models and memory. You won’t need to code or train them yourself.

03Keep becoming you.+

Your corrections and real outcomes should shape its later judgment. What it imagines stays separate from what really happened.

02 / THE FOUNDATION

A better AI starts
with a better
understanding of you.

∴

Our bet is simple. A separate personal foundation could make better decisions for you than directly personalizing a general model with the same small set of records.

That foundation can learn while the general model stays the same. New skills shouldn’t mean starting over with who you are.

We’re building and testing that idea. ↗

03 / YOURS MEANS YOURS

OWNERSHIP IS THE STARTING POINT

You should be
the owner
of you.

Sleight is being designed around that promise.

01Your personal AI.
You will own the personal models and memories Sleight creates.
02Your place.
Local storage is the default design. General skills may use outside models with your permission.
03Your say.
Inspect, correct and delete what it learns. These controls are part of the release we’re building toward.
